PRESSURE WASHER BUYING GUIDE

Best Pressure Washer for a Concrete Driveway

Cleaning a concrete driveway is a different job from washing a car or patio furniture. You need enough pressure to break up embedded dirt, but water flow matters just as much when you're trying to move all that grime across a large surface.

The goal isn't simply to buy the highest PSI number. The right pressure washer depends on your driveway, how often you clean it, your budget, and whether you want electric or gas power.

THE MOST IMPORTANT NUMBERS

PSI vs. GPM for driveway cleaning

PSI measures pressure. GPM measures water flow. For large concrete surfaces, both matter.

WasherMatch rule of thumb: For serious driveway cleaning, look for a machine with substantial water flow as well as adequate pressure. Greenworks currently recommends at least 2,500 PSI and 2.5 GPM for driveway cleaning.

A machine with high PSI but relatively low GPM can still work, but it may take longer to rinse and move loosened dirt across the surface. Higher-flow machines are generally better suited to large concrete areas.

BEFORE YOU BUY

Don't buy based on PSI alone

Two machines can advertise similar PSI numbers while performing very differently because their water-flow ratings are different.

For a small amount of routine cleaning, a homeowner electric machine can make sense. For large concrete areas, stubborn grime, or frequent cleaning, prioritize a machine with substantially more water flow.

Also consider the surface itself. Older or damaged concrete can be more vulnerable to aggressive pressure, so technique, nozzle selection, distance from the surface, and consistent movement matter.
